## PART 2: Scored Criteria

**Rating scale:**
- **1 – Absent:** No evidence in application materials (0% of weight)
- **2 – Claimed:** Stated but not demonstrated (33% of weight)
- **3 – Demonstrated:** Clear evidence in work sample or experience (67% of weight)
- **4 – Exceptional:** Standout quality or depth (100% of weight)
