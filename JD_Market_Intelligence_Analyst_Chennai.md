# Job Description: Market Intelligence Analyst (Entry Level)

**Location:** Chennai, India (Hybrid — 3 days in-office)
**Function:** Market Analytics / Growth Intelligence
**Seniority:** Entry Level / Junior (0–2 years)
**Employment Type:** Full-Time
**Compensation:** ₹3.5 – 5.5 LPA (based on skills and experience)

---

## About the Role

We are a digital marketing and market analytics company helping brands grow through data-driven decisions. As a Market Intelligence Analyst, you will be the first line of signal — tracking competitor moves, consumer shifts, and channel performance to surface insights that inform our clients' strategies.

This is a hands-on, output-oriented role. You will be working with real datasets from day one, owning your own dashboards, and presenting findings to cross-functional teams. The role sits at the intersection of market research, competitive intelligence, and performance analytics.

---

## What You Will Do

- Monitor competitor activity, campaign launches, pricing moves, and market positioning across digital channels
- Collect and structure data from multiple sources — web analytics platforms, social listening tools, industry reports, and internal CRM exports
- Build and maintain recurring performance dashboards tracking KPIs such as share of voice, keyword rankings, conversion trends, and audience segments
- Conduct consumer and category analysis to identify growth opportunities and market gaps
- Synthesise findings into clear, concise reports and slide decks for client-facing and internal stakeholders
- Support senior analysts on ad hoc research, A/B test analysis, and campaign attribution work
- Use AI-assisted tools (such as Claude or ChatGPT) to accelerate literature review, data summarisation, and first-draft reporting

---

## What We Are Looking For

**Non-negotiable:**
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Economics, Statistics, Business, or a related quantitative field (2024 or 2025 graduate preferred)
- Working proficiency in **SQL** for querying and joining structured datasets
- Comfortable in **Excel** — pivot tables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, basic modelling
- Ability to read data and produce a clearly written takeaway — not just describe numbers, but say what they mean

**Strong preference for:**
- Hands-on exposure to **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** or any web analytics platform
- Familiarity with at least one BI/visualisation tool: **Tableau**, **Power BI**, or **Looker**
- Basic **Python** for data manipulation (pandas) — not required, but a meaningful differentiator
- Experience with any market research or social listening tool (Semrush, Similarweb, Brandwatch, etc.)

**What makes someone stand out:**
- A portfolio project, internship output, or academic case study where you turned messy data into a clear recommendation
- Awareness of how AI tools are changing analytics workflows — not just awareness, but actual use
- Fast learner who reads about the industry on their own time
