# MIA — Marketing Intelligence & AI-Adoption Analyzer

Scores job descriptions against vocabulary learned from 4,949 LinkedIn postings.
Uses pre-computed TF-IDF group differences, NMF topic clusters, and a 42-skill
co-occurrence network to estimate whether a role sits in the AI-adjacent or
traditional segment of the marketing-analytics market.

Source: [Shiv-1801/LinkedIn-Market-Analysis](https://github.com/Shiv-1801/LinkedIn-Market-Analysis)
